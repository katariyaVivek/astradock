# AstraDock Roadmap

Milestones are sequential. A milestone is complete only when its lesson/model,
implementation, tests, and stated completion criteria are all satisfied. Future
items describe intent and must not be read as implemented capabilities.

Status vocabulary:

- **IMPLEMENTED**: completion criteria have been verified.
- **IN PROGRESS**: work exists but at least one completion criterion is not yet
  verified.
- **PLANNED**: no implementation claim is made.
